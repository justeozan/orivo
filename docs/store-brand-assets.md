# Store brand assets

The Store's mastheads show each brand's own logo and hardware. Every file in
`public/media/store-brands/` was taken from the maker's site or press kit on
2026-11-26, trimmed to the device and re-encoded as WebP with its transparency;
logos are the white versions, untouched apart from what is noted.

They are trademarks of their owners. Orivo shows them to say where a game is
sold, never to suggest the brand endorses Orivo.

## Terms to keep

- **Valve** (partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/branding): the Steam logo may
  not suggest Valve sponsors the product, may not be the most prominent thing on
  screen, and stands alone — never combined with other logos or words.
- **Epic** (logo guidelines on dev.epicgames.com): no recolouring, skewing or
  effects; the white version on dark grounds; clear space of one shield.
- Valve's newer hardware (Steam Machine, Steam Controller, Steam Frame) only had
  thumbnail-sized official renders, so the Steam masthead shows two Steam Decks.
  Epic sells no hardware: its masthead is its logo alone.
