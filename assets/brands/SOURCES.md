# Source marks — where each one comes from

Every segment of the Library's source row is a mark beside a name. The marks
are vectors drawn in `currentColor`, kept inline in the app
(`src/source-marks.ts`, and `src/icons.ts` for the marks it already carries),
never fetched. Every source is in the public domain, under CC0, or drawn for
Orivo: nothing here carries a copyleft or attribution licence into the app.

They are trademarks of their owners. Orivo shows them for one reason only: to
name the store a game comes from, or the console a plugin brings, in the
Library's source row. That is the use the marks exist for, and none of these
owners is affiliated with Orivo.

| Segment | Mark | Source | Terms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Steam, Epic Games, GOG, Ubisoft, Xbox | `src/icons.ts` | [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) | CC0 1.0 |
| Microsoft Store, Instant Gaming | `src/icons.ts` | The marks the store badges already wear; provenance noted beside each there | — |
| Android | `src/source-marks.ts` | [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/?q=android) 16.35.0, `android.svg` | CC0 1.0 |
| PSP | `src/source-marks.ts` | [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/?q=playstation) 16.35.0, `playstation.svg` | CC0 1.0 |
| Switch | `src/source-marks.ts` | The two Joy-Con of Wikimedia Commons' [Nintendo Switch Logo.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nintendo_Switch_Logo.svg) (`source/nintendo-switch.svg`), without the name under them | Public domain |

All Games, Local, Stream, a plugin and the retro consoles (NES, SNES, Game Boy,
GBA, Mega Drive) are Orivo's own groupings or have no mark Orivo can ship; they
take a glyph from `src/icons.ts` in the same place, at the same size.
