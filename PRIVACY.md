# Orivo Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 12, 2026_

Orivo is a game library made by Ozan Sahin. It brings the games you own on
other stores together in one place. This page explains what Orivo does with
your data. The short version: your library stays on your device, Orivo has no
account of its own, and there is no advertising and no tracking.

## What stays on your device

- **Your library**: the games Orivo found or that you added, their artwork,
  your favourites, your wishlist and your settings. They are stored in Orivo's
  own folder on your device and are never uploaded to us.
- **Your store sign-ins**: when you connect a store, the session or key that
  lets Orivo read your library is kept in your system's keychain (the iOS or
  macOS Keychain, or its equivalent on Windows, Linux and Android).
- **The keys you add in Settings** for IGDB, SteamGridDB or Google image
  search.

We do not run servers that receive your library, and we do not sell, rent or
share your data.

## When you connect a store

Signing in to Steam, Epic Games, GOG, Ubisoft Connect, Xbox, the Microsoft
Store or Instant Gaming happens on that store's own pages. Orivo then asks the
store for the list of games you own, directly from your device. What the store
does with your account is covered by its own privacy policy. You can
disconnect a store at any time in Settings › Libraries, which deletes the
stored sign-in.

## Information Orivo downloads

To show games, Orivo downloads public information from the stores and, if you
added your own keys, from IGDB, SteamGridDB and Google image search: titles,
descriptions, artwork and prices. Like any web request, these services see
your device's IP address. Orivo sends them game names or store identifiers,
not your personal information.

## Crash reports and feedback

Some builds of Orivo send crash reports to [Sentry](https://sentry.io), a
service that helps us find and fix bugs. A report contains technical details
only: the app version, the operating system and what went wrong in the code.
Orivo asks Sentry not to attach names or IP addresses to these reports. A small
share of sessions also sends performance measurements, such as how long a
screen took to load.

The Feedback button sends us the message you write. Adding your name, your
email address or a screenshot is optional and only happens if you choose to.
We use them only to answer you and to fix the problem.

## Children

Orivo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
information from them.

## Deleting your data

Disconnecting a store removes its sign-in. Uninstalling Orivo removes your
library and settings from your device. To have a crash report or a feedback
message deleted, write to us at the address below.
